About Me

I am an Assistant Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor at the School of Urban Planning and Design, Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School. My research lies at the intersection of urban climate, human mobility, and public health, with a particular focus on how extreme heat and heat exposure shape commuting, outdoor activities, everyday behavior, and health risks in cities.

My work explores how urban planning and design can help cities adapt to a warming climate, including cooling infrastructure, blue–green spaces, street trees and greenways, and the accessibility of public facilities. I am particularly interested in combining urban analytics, spatial data, and computational approaches to understand human–environment interactions and to support healthier and more climate-resilient cities.

Before joining Peking University, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Geography at King’s College London and a Lecturer with the London Interdisciplinary Social Science Doctoral Training Partnership (LISS DTP). I received my Ph.D. in Spatial Planning from TU Dortmund University, following earlier training in architecture and urban planning at Leibniz University Hannover and the China Academy of Art. Research Interests

My current research focuses on four closely connected areas:

Extreme heat & dynamic exposure

Measuring how heat and humid heat vary across the places and times people actually experience during commuting, outdoor work and everyday activity.

Urban climate · Dynamic exposure · Thermal comfort

Mobility & behavioural adaptation

Understanding how residents modify destinations, routes, activity timing and travel choices in response to extreme weather and unequal urban conditions.

Human mobility · Activity patterns · Travel behaviour

Health risk & environmental equity

Connecting environmental exposure with acute health risks and the unequal experiences of older adults, outdoor workers and other vulnerable groups.

Health geography · Vulnerability · Environmental justice

Cooling infrastructure & GeoAI

Using spatial data science to evaluate shade, street trees, blue-green infrastructure and public facilities, and to support climate-responsive planning.

GeoAI · Cooling interventions · Planning support

Research Assistant Opportunities

Urban Planning | Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School

Peking University ranks No. 13 globally and No. 1 in Mainland China in the QS World University Rankings 2027. The School of Urban Planning and Design at Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School conducts interdisciplinary research in urban planning, sustainability, climate, and spatial data science. We are now recruiting online Research Assistants to join ongoing research projects.

Research areas include: urban heat and thermal exposure, human mobility and activity patterns, climate and health risks, urban green and blue infrastructure, climate adaptation, GeoAI, GIS, remote sensing, and spatial data science.

Applicants with a Bachelor’s degree or above are welcome to apply. The position is primarily online and flexible. Open position

Postdoctoral Researcher Position in Urban Planning and Climate-Health Research

The School of Urban Planning and Design at Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School invites applications for a Postdoctoral Researcher to join the research group led by Dr. Jin Rui. The position is expected to start in November 2026.

The research group focuses on understanding how extreme heat and environmental exposure influence human mobility, daily activities, and health, and how urban planning and design interventions can contribute to healthier and more climate-resilient cities.

Research Areas

Potential research topics include:

  • Extreme heat, thermal exposure, and urban climate
  • Human mobility, commuting, outdoor activities, and behavioral adaptation
  • Environmental exposure and health risks
  • Cooling infrastructure and heat adaptation strategies
  • Blue-green infrastructure and climate-responsive urban design
  • GeoAI, GIS, remote sensing, street-view imagery, and spatiotemporal data analytics

Interdisciplinary applicants from urban planning, geography, environmental science, public health, GIScience, data science, and related fields are particularly welcome.

Position and Qualifications

  • Position: Postdoctoral Researcher
  • Expected start date: November 2026
  • Appointment: Two years initially, with possible extension subject to performance, funding availability, and mutual agreement
  • Applicants should hold, or be close to completing, a PhD in a relevant field
  • Experience in spatial analysis, quantitative methods, GeoAI, environmental exposure, human mobility, urban climate, or environmental health is highly desirable

The successful candidate will have opportunities to develop independent research, contribute to ongoing and emerging projects, work with multi-source urban and environmental datasets, and collaborate with interdisciplinary and international research teams.

Application

Applicants should submit:

  • A CV
  • A cover letter describing their research background and research interests

Applications should be sent to jin.rui@pku.edu.cn.

Application deadline: October 31, 2026

Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, and the position may be filled once a suitable candidate is identified. Early applications are encouraged.
